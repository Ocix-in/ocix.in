# Shy chef welcome · 20 October 2026

The resting badge uses the earlier circular chef portrait with the site's purple outline. On a fresh home-page arrival or explicit home reload, the chef emerges from the disc, waves and reveals a local-time greeting. After 2.5 seconds the card closes and the chef returns to the portrait. Moving the pointer over, tapping or focusing the card keeps it open for reading and following its links. Clicking the badge replays the welcome; the close button, Escape and outside dismissal also tuck the chef away.

The automatic welcome does not steal focus or interrupt an open menu/article, and stays quiet on product/information pages and internal links back to home. A manual opening before the automatic timer prevents a duplicate welcome on that load. Reduced motion switches directly between states without waving or sliding. The head artwork shares AVIF/WebP resources with the kitchen scene. No animation library or React hydration is shipped.
